SEASONAL PLANNER

Farming Calendar

General seasonal reference for India. Exact dates vary by district, crop variety and weather.

🌧️ Kharif · June–October

Rice, maize, cotton and other monsoon crops.

  • Prepare field and check seed quality before sowing.
  • Monitor weeds, pests and rainfall during growth.
  • Plan drainage during heavy rain.
  • Harvest at crop maturity in suitable weather.

❄️ Rabi · October–March

Wheat, mustard, chickpea and other winter crops.

  • Test soil and prepare the field.
  • Plan irrigation according to crop stage and soil moisture.
  • Monitor weeds and crop health.
  • Check maturity before harvesting.

☀️ Zaid · March–June

Selected vegetables, watermelon and summer crops.

  • Check water availability before planting.
  • Monitor heat stress and soil moisture.
  • Use locally recommended varieties.
  • Harvest at suitable maturity.
